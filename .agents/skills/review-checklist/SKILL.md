# Review checklist

Read the brief the user points to in full. If you cannot locate or access it, ask the user to provide it.

1. **Ownership.** Check whether the brief explicitly identifies who is accountable. A named person or an unambiguous role or team can satisfy the check if the brief assigns ownership to them.
2. **Success criteria.** Check whether the brief describes an observable result and how to tell whether it was achieved. Flag vague goals and measures that leave the success threshold unclear.
3. **Scope consistency.** Compare the opening scope with the proposal, deliverables, and success criteria. Flag unexplained changes to the audience, work, or boundaries. Clearly labeled exclusions and future work can coexist with a consistent scope.
4. **Problem before fix.** Check whether the brief explains what is wrong and who is affected before it proposes a fix. Flag an opening that leads with a solution or only implies the problem.

Return one finding per check with a **Pass**, **Needs clarification**, or **Missing** status. Cite the relevant section or a short excerpt when available. For gaps, give a concrete suggestion grounded in the brief and identify any information the author needs to supply. Leave the brief as written unless the user asks for edits.
